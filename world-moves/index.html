World Moves Privacy Policy

Developer: Digital Literacy Studio
App: World Moves (Android, package studio.digitalliteracy.worldmoves)
Effective date: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how the World Moves app handles your information. World Moves is a critical thinking and writing practice app, the companion to the book Writing the World.

Summary

World Moves does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. The app does not send your answers, progress or any other information off your device.

Information stored on your device

Your progress in the app (such as your streak, your rank and which drills you have seen) is saved only on your own device so the app can remember where you are. It is never uploaded. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of the app's data are turned off, so this progress is not copied to any cloud service. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because the app does not collect any.

Permissions

The app's Android manifest includes the internet permission only because the app's framework loads its own built-in pages through a local secure address. World Moves does not use this permission to contact any server.

Children

World Moves is designed for students preparing for A-level General Paper and for adult learners. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact Digital Literacy Studio using the developer email address shown on the World Moves page in the Google Play Store.
