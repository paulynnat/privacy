This page is the privacy policy for Five in a Row, the Android app published by Digital Literacy Studio on Google Play.

About the app

Five in a Row (The Moves Playbooks: Five in a Row) is a free Gomoku game with short reading cards for the Story Moves, World Moves and Five Moves tracks. It is made for all ages, including children, and is designed to meet Google Play's Families policy.

What the app collects and stores

The app collects no personal information from children or anyone else. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data, and it requests no Android permissions. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

It saves only your game progress and settings (levels completed, board size and rule), and that stays on your device. Android cloud backup of the app's data is turned off. You can clear your progress at any time in the app, or by uninstalling it.

Because we do not collect any data, we do not share or sell data, and we do not share data with third parties.
