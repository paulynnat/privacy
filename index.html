Privacy Policy

Developer: Digital Literacy Studio
Effective date: 28 September 2026

This page is the privacy policy for the Android apps published by Digital Literacy Studio on Google Play. It covers each app listed below. The first section applies to all of them, and each app's own section describes exactly what that app stores.

Story MovesWorld MovesFive Moves

All Digital Literacy Studio apps

Our apps do not collect, share or sell any personal or usage data. They have no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. Nothing you do in the apps is sent to us or to anyone else.

Any progress an app remembers is saved only on your own device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Each app's Android manifest includes the internet permission only because the app's framework loads its own built-in pages through a local secure address. None of the apps use this permission to contact a server.

Because we do not collect any data, we do not share data with third parties.

Story Moves

Package studio.digitalliteracy.storymoves

Story Moves is a choose-your-own-adventure creative writing app for primary school children. It is designed to meet Google Play's Families policy.

The app collects no personal information from children or anyone else. It has no ads, no in-app links to outside websites, and no third-party SDKs. The only thing it saves is story progress, such as which endings the child has found, and that stays on the device.

World Moves

Package studio.digitalliteracy.worldmoves

World Moves is a critical thinking and writing practice app for students preparing for A-level General Paper, and the companion to the book Writing the World.

The app saves your progress (your streak, your rank and which drills you have seen) only on your device. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of the app's data are turned off, so this progress is not copied to any cloud service.

Five Moves

Package studio.digitalliteracy.fifthmove

Five Moves is a practice app for adults on reading the fine print before big decisions.

The app saves your practice progress only on your device and never sends it to us. If you have turned on Google's backup for your own device, Android may include this progress in your personal device backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings and is not accessible to us.

Children

Story Moves is made for children and collects no personal information. World Moves and Five Moves are made for older students and adults. None of our apps knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If we add a new app, it will be added to this page before it is released.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact Digital Literacy Studio using the developer email address shown on the app's page in the Google Play Store.
